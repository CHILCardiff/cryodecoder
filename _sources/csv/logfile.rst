CSV format
==========

The ``.csv`` files produced by the serial and file decoders are generated by :py:class:`cryodecoder.parser.CSVLogger` classs. They aim to provide descriptions of the key parameters from different levels of blocks and faclitate quick analysis of the instrument data by providing a comma-separated list of raw and decoded parameter values. The fields are listed below in :numref:`Table %s <tab_cryodecoder_csv_fields>` in the order they appear in the CSV file.
